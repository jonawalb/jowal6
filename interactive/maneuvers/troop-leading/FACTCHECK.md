| 4 | The steps are not rigid; 1-3 normally in order; 3 to 7 as needed; 8 throughout | "The steps are not rigid. Some steps are done concurrently, while others may go on continuously throughout planning and preparation. | 7 | One-third / two-thirds rule (leader keeps a third, subordinates the rest) | "There is no specific timeline allocated to mission analysis, but leaders should always adhere to the one-third/two-thirds rule, allocating roughly one-third of available planning and preparation time to themselves, and allowing their subordinates the remaining two-thirds." | ATP 3-21.8 (C1) 2-24, p. 2-12 |

## Not doctrine (labelled so on the page)
Twelve hours, the leader's three-hour need, the squads' plan/check/rehearse list and lengths, the casualty rule, step shares, the platoon sergeant's move as step 4, the reconnaissance walk length, who fires at whom, and the lighting of the steps one after another (the page says the order is a guide).
