# Phasing and Transitions: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (JP 5-0 2011 p. III-41; JP 5-0 2017 p. iii; JP 3-0 2017 Ch1 2018 V-6.a–d, pp. V-12 to V-14; JP 5-0 2020 pp. IV-37 to IV-38). All quotes confirmed. Fixed after the check: the transition sentence now quotes the "marks a change between phases or … branch or sequel" wording instead of turning a description into a definition; "planned shifts" is marked as the bench simplification; the "looser" claim is attributed to JP 3-0 (2018) (JP 5-0 2020 says phases are planned to be sequential and do not overlap); "recast as notional" became "as notional phases"; the JP 3-0 (2022) sentence is softened. Correction: V-6.d(1) is on p. V-14, not V-13.
