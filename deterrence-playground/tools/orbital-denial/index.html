Notional model. Both powers and their satellites are invented. The debris physics and the 2007 and 2021 ASAT test anchors are real.

How to play

You command the satellites of Blue, a notional power, through a ten-month crisis that turns into war with Red. Both sides rely on four constellations: reconnaissance, communications, navigation, and early warning with nuclear command. Each month, pick two actions in the panel, then press End month. Red moves at the same time.

Reversible attacks (jam, dazzle, cyber) wear off. Destructive ones (missile, co-orbital) kill satellites for good, but a missile kill leaves a debris cloud that also threatens your own satellites and everyone else's in that orbit. Touching early-warning satellites raises the risk of nuclear escalation. You win if your space support beats Red's over the war without crossing the nuclear threshold.
