# Mobile Defense: fact check

## VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90, 1 May 2023; 8-9, 10-1, 10-2, 10-6 to 10-9, 10-15, 10-34). All 12 claims confirmed; "typically" and "may" are kept on the one-half to two-thirds figure. The owner chose a rear strike (2026-10-13); the page labels it the model’s choice, because FM 3-90 does not specify where the striking force hits. Also "small" became "minimum combat power".

Source for every claim: Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023.

Builder's notes for the checker:
- Item 8 keeps the manual's qualifier: the page says the enemy is "generally allowed to penetrate in at least one place" (paraphrase, not a quote).
- Not doctrinal claims: every number on the bench (100, 70, 30%, 49, 0.7, 34.3, the 50–65% band, timings) is notional and labeled so on the page and in METHOD.md. The page states the constants were tuned so the band lands near para 10-2's typical split.
- The guide walls and obstacle studs as fixing-force parts follow 10-7 ("canalizing attacking enemy forces into engagement areas"; most countermobility assets) and 10-4 (obstacles shape penetrations); the page does not quote these.
