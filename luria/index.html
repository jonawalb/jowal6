How results come in

  • Polls close at 7:00 PM ET. Localities then report precinct by precinct, so “Precincts in” climbs through the night.
  • Early, mailed and Election Day ballots are counted separately. Each type can arrive at a different time, so an early lead can shrink or grow. Watch “How people voted.”
  • Mailed ballots can keep arriving after tonight. Totals can still change in the days after the election, and results are unofficial until certified.
  • Virginia Beach is the big one. It has 108 of the district’s 222 precincts.
  • Where the numbers come from. The Virginia Department of Elections results site. Its data can’t be read directly by other websites, so a relay copies the VA-2 numbers about once a minute for this page.
