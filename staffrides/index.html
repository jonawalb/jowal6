Virtual staff rides

Walk the ground. Make the call. Check the sources.

A staff ride takes officers to a battlefield to study a campaign where it happened: standing on the ground, seeing what the commanders saw, and arguing about what they should have done. These virtual rides rebuild that experience from elevation data, period maps and the documentary record.

Every unit move, number and quotation is tied to a source, and the places where the sources disagree are shown, not smoothed over.

  1. Study. The campaign, the forces and the ground, day by day on a timeline.
  2. Stands. At each stop you take a commander's place, with the options they actually weighed. Pick one, then see what they chose.
  3. Terrain. Line-of-sight views computed from real elevation data, in 2D or 3D.
  4. Integration. Logistics, diplomacy, the debated counterfactuals and a short lens from the literature.
