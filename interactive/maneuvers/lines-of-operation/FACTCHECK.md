# Lines of Operation and Effort: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (JP 5-0, 1 Dec 2020; ch. IV 6.a, pp. IV-29 to IV-32; GL-11). All quotes are exact. Fixed after the check: the lede and meta no longer state the one-terminal mechanic as doctrine ("in this model", "serving one purpose"); LOOs are described with the source terms (geographic or force-oriented objective); "conditions no map shows" became "where position matters little"; "fixed order" became "generally in sequence"; the bar layout is marked "in this model"; the antecedent "LOEs and mission areas" was restored; "can be" became "may be".

Source for every claim: Joint Chiefs of Staff, JP 5-0, *Joint Planning*, 1 December 2020.
