        How to read it

        Pick a mineral. The top chart follows it through the supply chain: mining, then separation or refining, then a component where public data exist (rare-earth magnets). Each column splits one stage's world output by country. Bands link the same country across stages; they do not show trade between countries, which no public source reports at this level.

        Pick a shock in the side panel. The country's output at the stages it withholds is hatched, and the simulator below shows what reaches buyers elsewhere, month by month: the narrowest stage sets the flow, inventories and any stockpile cover the gap first, and the red area is the shortfall left. Idle plants restart and new plants come on line after the lead times you set. Scrub the month slider to read any point.

        The heat table compares every mineral and stage at once. The end-use cards say where each mineral goes in defense systems, quoting the government source.
