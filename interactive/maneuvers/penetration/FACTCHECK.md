# Penetration and Exploitation: fact check

**VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90, 1 May 2023, Wayback copy). All quotes are exact (paras 2-36, 2-45, 2-47). Fixed after the check: the lede now gives both triggers from 2-36; the caption says "normally planned"; "shoulders" is glossed as the doctrinal "flanks"; the lede says the bench runs the phases in one order (para 2-51: after the breach the situation sets the order)..** The builder has not verified these claims; an independent checker should confirm each one against the source.

Source opened this session (2026-10-13): Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023 (supersedes FM 3-90-1 and FM 3-90-2, 22 March 2013).

Not doctrinal claims (stated as the bench model on the page): the shoulder pins as a mechanism, the springs shutting the gap and cutting the carriage off, all blow counts, widths and timings. The page says doctrine names the task of securing the flanks but does not describe any such mechanism.

Checker notes:
- Claim 3 is quoted as a list; the first phase is paraphrased ("breach the main defensive positions" in the caption, "breaching the enemy force's main defensive positions" in the text, which is the manual's exact bullet).
- The page says "hold its shoulders" / "shoulder pins" for the bench parts. FM 3-90 uses "flanks" of the penetration, not "shoulders"; the doctrine section quotes "flanks".
