# Breaching (SOSRA): fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check against the official FM 3-90 (1 May 2023) and ATP 3-21.10 (1 June 2026) PDFs. No quote was wrong. Findings applied: "fixed order" changed to "set order"; 18-23 cited as pp. 18-4 to 18-5; "in this model" and "notional counts" added to the no-suppression outcome; the strict station chain and the overlap statement marked as the figure's own, with F-27 as the only doctrinal ordering claim; aria-valuetext marks counts notional; table 18-1 removed from the sources line. ATP 3-90.4 is named only as not opened.
Sources opened this session (2026-10-14):
- Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023. Official: https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN38160-FM_3-90-000-WEB-1.pdf (text extracted from the PDF). The 2026 ATP 3-21.10 still lists it as "FM 3-90. Tactics. 1 May 2023"; no newer edition was found.

Official: https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN46667-ATP_3-21.10-000-WEB-1.pdf. Appendix F, "Combined Arms Breaching". 2018 paragraph numbers (G-xx) are NOT used.
- ATP 3-90.4/MCTP 3-34A, *Combined Arms Mobility*, 10 June 2022 (date as listed in the ATP 3-21.10 reference list): NOT opened (no public copy found). It appears only through FM 3-90 and ATP 3-21.10, which cite it.
