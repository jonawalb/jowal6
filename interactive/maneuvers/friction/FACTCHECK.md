VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (Clausewitz, On War, trans. Graham 1873, Bk I ch. 7 paras 2–4, Gutenberg #1946; MCDP 1, 20 June 1997, pp. 5–6). All seven claims confirmed; the page quotes Graham, not Howard & Paret. Fixed after the check: the page now says outright that the discrete grit contradicts Clausewitz’s "not concentrated … at a few points", and drops the "steady drag" gloss.

Notes for the checker:
- The page deliberately quotes Graham, not Howard and Paret (under copyright). The page makes no claim about the Howard and Paret wording.
- Paragraph numbers count the chapter's paragraphs from its first ("As long as we have no personal knowledge of War...").
- In the Gutenberg text the sentence after claim 1 reads "...who has not seen War, Suppose now a traveller..." (comma, apparently a transcription slip). The page ends the quote at "War." and does not reproduce the slip.

Not factual claims: the pull formula mg(sin θ + μ cos θ) is standard mechanics; the static excess, spring, dashpot, winch, grit and paper-time numbers are notional and labeled so on the page and in METHOD.md.
