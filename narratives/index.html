Narrative Tracking

What governments say, week by week. These trackers follow the phrases that foreign ministries, defense ministries and leaders keep coming back to, and when the language shifts. Every count leads back to the statement it came from.

Every tracker is open to everyone, including Rhetoric Search, which covers official statements and state and other media from more countries.
