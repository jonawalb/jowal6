Interactive Deterrence

Maps, models and explainers on deterrence, nuclear weapons, war and information warfare. Some tools turn my working papers into models you can play with; others put open data on strikes, shipping, arsenals and trade in one place. Every figure is sourced, and anything a model has to assume is marked as notional.
