# Mission Command: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (ADP 6-0, 31 July 2019; paras 1-14, 1-26, 1-58, 1-62; archived FAS copy). All quotes verbatim; the seven principles are exact and in order. Fixed after the check: the 1-58 paraphrase restores "direction and guidance … to focus their activities"; the 1-62 sentence is no longer a causal chain; the set-point mapping is marked "in the model"; the meta says the engine model is notional.
