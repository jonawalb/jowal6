Not factual claims (labeled notional on the page and in METHOD.md): the force sizes (16 vs 10 + 10), α = 0.06, β = 0.02, the 4-step shift, the half-man cut-off, the outcomes and casualty counts they produce, the linear law's area-fire form (stated as the model's choice), the balance geometry and tilt mapping, and every battlefield size, distance and timing. "Real units usually break or withdraw long before" is the page's own general statement, uncited.
