# Reconnaissance Pull: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90, 1 May 2023, paras 12-76 to 12-79, p. 12-18). All quotes verbatim, qualifiers kept. Fixed after the check: all four considerations from 12-76 are now listed separately; "plan branches and adapt" became "plan branches and decision points (para. 12-77)"; push is credited with "detailed, well-rehearsed planning" (12-78) and "neither is wrong" is marked as the page’s reading; the meta no longer says push follows "a plan made in advance".

Source opened this session: Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023.
Official URL: https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN38160-FM_3-90-000-WEB-1.pdf

Lind (*Maneuver Warfare Handbook*) and Simpkin (*Race to the Swift*) were not opened and are not cited.
