# Operational Pause: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (JP 5-0, 1 Dec 2020; GL-11, IV-37 to IV-39). All quotes verbatim, qualifiers kept. Fixed after the check: the lede gives the full purpose ("regenerate combat power or augment sustainment"); the pump threshold and the enemy-recovery inference are marked "in this model"; the "cuts both ways" gloss became a direct "doctrine warns"; the meta says notional model with quotes from JP 5-0.
