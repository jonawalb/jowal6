## Not doctrine (labelled "in this model" or "notional" on the page, in METHOD.md and in the aria labels)
Four moves and four phase lines; every time (3.0 s a move, 1.6 s a resupply leg, 1.2 s by vehicle and 2.6 s by litter bearers per casualty, 1.6 s for the aid and litter pair); the casualty counts and where they fall; the ammunition tank (35 per cent low); the six enemy and when they go down; the rule that the line does not step off until the ammunition arrives; the single carrier doing both jobs in the order casualties first, ammunition second (the manual says "carries additional ammunition forward ... and evacuates casualties", and ATP 4-02.13 describes a vehicle that delivers ammunition and backhauls casualties); the platoon moving as three squads in a line; the picture's farm track and truck; the run ending at the fourth phase line.
