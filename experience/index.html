Things I Do

Taiwan Security Monitor

Associate Director

When I first started working with Taiwan Security Monitor, it was an academic project. There were six undergrads covering current events and looking for points of interest. Together with Dr. Michael Hunzeker, we transformed the project into what TSM is today. Less than a year later, we had a team of fourteen people who were briefing the Department of Defense and the Intelligence Community on our geospatial and open-source analysis.

Today, we have more than twenty-five researchers and analysts. We have over 40,000 followers, and our analysis regularly supports work across the Intelligence Community and Department of Defense, with our public-facing work cited by dozens of global media outlets. I am responsible for three teams of analysts, training and onboarding for the project, funding efforts, and our public platform. I also led the development of our artificial intelligence-driven early warning system, which tracks military exercises and naval movements in the Indo-Pacific. I've designed wargames used with the U.S. Army War College and other defense partners.
