# Lifting and Shifting Fires: fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check against FM 3-90 and ATP 3-21.8 PDFs. Findings applied: "Halted at MSL" reworded to "MSL hold (model)" with ATP 3-21.8 A-29 "shift (or cease)"; walking fires onto the objective's rear labelled the drawing's choice; "notional" added beside the 50 m and 150 m distances. "Check fire" (FM 3-09) was not opened and the page does not use the term.

1. FM 3-09 (12 Aug 2024) could not be opened (armypubs URL returns an error page, no archive copy). Whether "check fire" is a defined term there is UNVERIFIED. Neither FM 3-90 nor ATP 3-21.8 defines it, and the page does not use it. Sources use CEASE FIRE as a command (ATP 3-21.8 C-121 area, FM 3-90 line "Cease fire, given to cease all fires"; A-29 "shifting fires (or ceasing)").
2. Walking fires onto the rear of the objective is NOT doctrine. C-121: "Shift fires (commonly called walking fires) forward of the assault line." A-29: "shifting fire forward of the assault element as it moves across the objective or to the flanks of targets or areas." The page's "The guns walk their fire onto the rear of the objective until the assault arrives" must be labeled the drawing's choice.
3. "Halted at MSL" and "A cease fire at the line halts the assault for 2.5 figure seconds": no doctrine describes a halt. A-29 says observers call fires to "shift (or cease)". Proposed wording: "At the minimum safe line the fires cease (ATP 3-21.8, A-29); the pause in the assault is the figure's own device." Readout: "MSL hold (model)".
4. Machine-view text states "50 m" and "150 m" without "notional" in that paragraph; only the caption and the notional section cover it. Add "notional" beside the 50 m stop.
5. Claim 4: FM 3-90 5-27 lists the FSCM and signals bullets within the fire-plan characteristics list; "asks for" is a paraphrase, acceptable.

Interpretations to check (the figure's own reading, labeled as such or drawn, not quoted):
- "Walking fire forward of the assault line" is drawn as the guns moving onto the rear of the objective between the lift and the assault's arrival, then shifting to depth (Shift) or stopping (Lift).
- In the late case the figure lifts the fires at the minimum safe line and halts the assault briefly (readout "Halted at MSL"). A-29 says observers call for fires to shift or cease as the element approaches the line; the halt is the model's way to show the cost of fires kept on too long, not a doctrinal procedure.
- The mortars and the guns lift together at the final coordination line in this model; doctrine's echelonment of fires (ATP 3-21.8, B-12 to B-16) shifts systems in order of risk estimate distance. Not claimed on the page.

Notional, not claims: the 50 m minimum safe line, 160 m start, 32 m per figure second, the recovery curve (10% + 90% × (1 − e^(−t/4))), the 2.5 s halt, the depth team's 0.8 s delay and 20-point share, the risk index, all losses, the unit sizes and positions drawn (enemy squad in four two-man holes, a four-man depth team, a support-by-fire element of two MG teams and a rifle squad), and the 1.5× figure scale. The page labels them notional.
