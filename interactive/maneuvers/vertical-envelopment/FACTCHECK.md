# Vertical Envelopment: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90 2023 paras 2-58, 2-61; FM 3-99 2015 paras 1-114, 2-24, 4-18). All 10 claims confirmed. Fixed after the check: "limited tactical mobility"; "can conduct double and vertical envelopments"; FM 3-99 "gives the joint definition, citing JP 3-18"; the resupply line attributed to FM 3-99; the lede tied to 4-18’s linkup or withdrawal..
