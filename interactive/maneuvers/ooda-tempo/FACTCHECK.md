Not factual claims (notional, labeled on the page and in METHOD.md): cycle times, the 0.6 s-per-hour scale, equal stage lengths, the stale rule, the long-run share formula and its quoted values (0%, 50%, 100%, 12 hours per lap), gear tooth counts.

Wording notes for the checker:
- The lede paraphrases claim 1 ("argued for operating at a faster tempo … and, better yet, getting 'inside' its cycle").
- The page calls the bench's circle "the popular, simplified loop, not Boyd's drawing". Hammond (p. 383) also says the OODA Loop "does not appear" in the 1987 *Discourse*, but the phrase "observation–orientation–decision–action … loop" does appear in the 1987 *Patterns of Conflict* (claim 1), so the page claims only that the 1995 sketch is Boyd's final drawing, not his only or first use of the idea.
