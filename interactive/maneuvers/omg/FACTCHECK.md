# Operational Maneuver Group: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 100-2-1, 16 July 1984, pp. 4-5 to 4-9, Wayback copy). All quotes match. Fixed after the check: "disrupting, capturing or seizing other objectives"; the lede no longer says "a new kind of force" (the manual calls it an updated older concept) or "had been developing"; the condition is "most likely to be used", not "depended on"; the meta says "as the U.S. Army described it in 1984"; "not used everywhere" became "did not expect one everywhere"; the track-length note is loosened.
