# Bypassing Strongpoints: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90, 1 May 2023; B-9 to B-13, pp. B-3 to B-4; 12-27, p. 12-6). All quotes exact; the five factors and two techniques are complete; "Generally" is kept. Fixed after the check: the caption says "until relieved by another force"; the meta says "Inspired by" and "enemy force" instead of "Built from" and "reduce"; the approval inference now quotes B-11 directly; the bypassed-force gloss became "Bypassing has a cost"; the floats staying behind are marked "in this model".
