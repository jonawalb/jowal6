Maneuver Trainer: Hard

Real time. Read the briefing, start the clock, and command: select a unit, give it an order and tap the ground or the enemy. The clock does not stop. At company level your orders go by radio and take a little while to reach the platoons, so order early.

Pick a scenario

    How to play

    1. Briefing. Mission, enemy, terrain, troops and time. Your troops are blue; the enemy appears in red only once one of your soldiers has seen it, and a faint red chalk ring marks where it was last seen.
    2. Start the clock. It runs at real time and cannot be paused. “Stop exercise” ends the run and goes to the review.
    3. Select a unit. Tap it on the map or in the roster, or press its number key.
    4. Give an order. Pick it from the order bar or with its key: M move, S support by fire, A assault, H hold / defend, Y bypass, K smoke, F call for fire, X breach, B break contact, R reserve, O observe / reconnoiter, C counterattack, L lift fire, T shift fire. Then tap the place or the enemy. Tapping open ground with a unit selected and no order picked is a move.
    5. Routes. Turn on Waypoints (or hold Shift) to lay a route of several points; tap the last point again, press Enter or Send to give it. For an assault or support by fire, taps on open ground are waypoints and the tap on the enemy sends the order; tap the same spot twice to fire on an area.
    6. Radio delay. At company level an order reaches a platoon after a notional 10 to 20 seconds (radio, then the platoon leader issuing it). A chalk route and a countdown show the order on its way.
    7. Review. The score ranks the objective first, then your losses, then time. The after-action review says what happened, why, and what to sustain or improve, with links to the figures. “Show the recommended plan” draws one good answer on the map.

    What is notional

    The scenarios, places and enemy are fictional. Every number (ranges, rates of fire, suppression, casualties, the radio delay) is a notional teaching value chosen so the lesson of each scenario shows, not data and not a model of real weapons. Casualties fall on both sides even when a plan works; soldiers who are down are shown greyed and still, and are carried back to a casualty collection point. Soldiers, trees and positions are drawn larger than scale so they read. This is not a US Army training aid.

    Terms follow Headquarters, Department of the Army, ATP 3-21.8, Infantry Rifle Platoon and Squad (11 January 2024, incorporating Change 1, June 2025): the fire team (team leader, automatic rifleman, grenadier, rifleman, para. 1-11), the squad as two fire teams and a squad leader (para. 1-9), fire and movement in contact (para. 1-9), support by fire (para. 3-56); and FM 3-90, Tactics (1 May 2023) for the forms of maneuver (para. 2-24).
