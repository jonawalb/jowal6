# Surfaces and Gaps: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (MCDP 1, 20 June 1997, pp. 92–93; Wayback copy). All 10 claims confirmed, with qualifiers kept. Fixed after the check: the causal "so" before "actively seek out gaps" became "and the manual says"; the lede says the main effort may shift "to the unit that found one".

Notes for the checker:
- The related "pull … rather than pushing" sentence that follows claim 9 on p. 93 is deliberately not quoted here; it belongs to sibling figure #19 (recon-pull).
- The page's statements that the bench "does not create gaps" and "cannot be fooled by a disguised surface", that the deepest blade "stands in" for the unit that located a gap, and that "in this model a deeper probe means a better gap" are statements about the bench model, not doctrine.

Not doctrinal claims: the 6 mm gap threshold, the 16 mm blade reach, the 24 mm ram reach, the four faces, the tie rule and all timings are notional and labeled so on the page and in METHOD.md.

Notional, not claims (labeled so on the page): figure scale (about 1.5× the ground); every size, range and timing; the enemy's strength (a rifle pair per lane, a machine-gun team in the centre lane, a squad leader) and its positions in depth; the mapping of a key's set-back to how far back that lane's element sits; the follow-on element's make-up (a fire team, its squad leader, the platoon leader and his radio operator); that the teams held on surfaces stay down and keep firing while the follow-on element goes through; wire between the lanes; the casualty counts and who goes down.
