Removed: the JP 3-0 (17 January 2017, C1) claim that joint doctrine uses nearly the same words with "primary efforts" (appendix A, para 2e(1)). Not doctrinal claims: the 12 discs, the breach at 6, the hold at 1, gate travel and loader speeds are notional and labeled so on the page and in METHOD.md. "The loader stands for that shift" and "breach / held / exposed" are statements about the bench model.
