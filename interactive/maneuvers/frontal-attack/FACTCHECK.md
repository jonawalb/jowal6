# Frontal Attack: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (FM 3-90, 1 May 2023; para 2-24 with Table 2-1, 2-27, 2-28, 2-31). All quotes are exact, with qualifiers kept. A whole-manual search found no "3:1" and no numeric ratio thresholds; the page now says so for the whole manual.

Source opened this session: Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023. Official URL https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN38160-FM_3-90-000-WEB-1.pdf (did not resolve from this machine); read from the Wayback copy of the same file, https://web.archive.org/web/20250224155209/https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN38160-FM_3-90-000-WEB-1.pdf, which the page links.

| # | Claim on the page | Where on the page | Source | Para / page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FM 3-90 lists five forms of maneuver, frontal attack first | Doctrine section | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-24 and Table 2-1, pp. 2-12 to 2-13 |
| 2 | Frontal attack: “a form of maneuver in which an attacking force seeks to destroy a weaker enemy force or fix a larger enemy force in place over a broad front” | Doctrine section | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-27, p. 2-13 (also Glossary, “*frontal attack”) |
| 3 | “A frontal attack is necessary when assailable flanks do not exist.” | Doctrine section | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-28, p. 2-14 |
| 4 | Keeps “continuous pressure along the entire front until a breach occurs or the attacking forces succeed in pushing the enemy forces back” (source sentence begins “Frontal attack maintain…”; quoted from “continuous”) | Doctrine section; card caption | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-28, p. 2-14 |
| 5 | Units “rarely conduct frontal attack when they lack overwhelming combat power unless fixing the enemy is vital to a larger operation’s success” | Doctrine section; paraphrased in lede | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-28, p. 2-14 |
| 6 | It “is seldom possible for a unit to exert sufficient pressure to overwhelm an enemy force using a frontal attack, since it strikes the enemy force along a significant portion of the enemy’s front” | Doctrine section | FM 3-90 (2023) | Para 2-31, p. 2-15 |
| 7 | FM 3-90 gives no such (ratio) thresholds for a frontal attack | What is notional | FM 3-90 (2023) | Paras 2-27 to 2-35 contain no ratios (negative claim; checker should confirm) |

Not doctrinal claims: the 1:1 and 4.5:1 thresholds, the linear push-back, the one-sixth share per block and the choice of which block gives are notional and labeled so on the page.

Notional, not claims (labeled so on the page): figure scale (about 1.5× the ground), every size, range and timing, the enemy's strength (a rifle squad and a machine-gun team in six positions), the ratios at which each blue element joins (machine-gun team 1:1, second squad 1.5:1, second machine-gun team 2.5:1, third squad 3:1), the second enemy line and how far it falls back, which position gives at the breach, and the terrain (stream and wood on the flanks). The drawing does not count men to the ratio. "Line of departure", "squad line" and "squad column" name what is drawn.
