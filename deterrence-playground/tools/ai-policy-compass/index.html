How to read it

The tool sets each government's own texts side by side. It covers national policy: executive orders, laws, regulations and the standards that carry them out, national strategies and plans, and the statements governments make at the UN. It leaves out what companies, provinces or US states do except where a debate turns on them, and it does not judge how well any rule is enforced.

"Status" means legal or policy status as of the date above. A US executive order revoked by a later one shows as hollow, with an arc from the order that revoked it. Chinese regulations that are still in force but have been extended by later measures show as solid, with an "implements" arc from the newer text. Non-binding strategies and UN resolutions count as current unless a later text replaced them.
