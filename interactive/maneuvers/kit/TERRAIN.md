# Battlefield terrain kit (`kit/terrain.mjs`)

The Battlefield view of a Maneuvers figure draws **the ground and the troops**, not a model of them: full-bleed isometric terrain with no board, plate, frame or feet, tiny soldiers in their real groups and formations, enemy positions, tracer fire and chalk control measures. It replaces the sand-table counters of `kit/field.mjs` (the owner rejected those as "the anatomical ones but with soldiers on the anatomy layer"). Reference implementation: **Fig 1, `flank-attack/`** (`scripts/field-scene.mjs`, `scripts/build.mjs`, `scripts/page.html`). Copy it.

All functions are pure and take the iso kit as `K` and a projection `P`, so `build.mjs` calls them in Node and the page inlines the file (imports and exports stripped) and calls them per frame. `terrain.mjs` imports helpers from `field.mjs`, so inline **`field.mjs`, then `terrain.mjs`, then your `field-scene.mjs`**. Only `trStage` and `trFire` touch the DOM (page only). Ground level is `z = 0`; anything standing takes `z = T.height(x, y)`.

## Pick the scale first

The unit you draw is the unit the concept is about. Decide it before any geometry, and put the choice in METHOD.md.

| Concept is about | Draw | Helpers | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire and movement, fixing, flanking, assaulting a position, bounding, a squad or platoon battle drill | **Soldiers**: fire teams (4), squads (9), a platoon (about 30) | `trFireTeam`, `trSquad`, `trPlatoon`, `trSoldier` | Fix and Flank, frontal attack, infiltration, surfaces and gaps, combined-arms dilemma at small-unit level, recon pull |
| Company and battalion maneuver: envelopment, penetration, turning movement, mobile and elastic defense, bypass, hammer and anvil | **Clusters**: one cluster of figures or vehicles per platoon, three per company | `trCompany`, `trBattalion`, `trVehicle` (mechanized/armor), `trSoldier` (infantry) | double envelopment, penetration, mobile defense, hammer and anvil, bypass, retrograde |
| Operational ideas: centre of gravity, decisive points, culminating point, operational reach, lines of operation, phasing, deep battle, OMG, interior lines | **Battalion clusters on a larger ground**, roads and rail as lines of communication, depots as buildings | `trBattalion`, `trRoad`, `trHouse` clusters, `trChalk` for boundaries and phase lines | culminating point, operational reach, deep battle, OMG, interior lines |

When the reading is uncertain (which echelon fixes, where the objective sits), build the most doctrinal reading you can source and list the question for the owner. Sizes, ranges and timings are notional on every figure; say so on the page. Figures are drawn about 1.5× too large for the ground so a soldier still reads at 390 px; say that too.

## API

**Tokens.** `TERRAIN_CSS`: append after `ISO_CSS + FIELD_CSS`. Muted earth tints (`--tr-ground`, `--tr-meadow`, `--tr-field-a/b`, `--tr-road`, `--tr-wood`, `--tr-canopy`, `--tr-water`, `--tr-high` for hypsometric tops, `--tr-slope-0..3`, `--tr-stone`, `--tr-bag`, `--tr-pit`), affiliation tints for soldiers (`--tr-f-*` blue, `--tr-h-*` red; `-t` top, `-l` sunlit side, `-d` shaded side, `-g` gear, `-ink` outline), `--tr-lit-ink` (the main effort's outline: black on paper) and tracer colours `--tr-f-fire`, `--tr-h-fire`. Light values ship; the dark block keeps the kit's dark theme usable.

**Relief.** `trTerrain({ rises: [{ cx, cy, r, steps, h, top, seed, wobble, squash, lean }] })` → `T` with `T.height(x, y)` (sum of the terraces containing the point) and `T.rises`. Each rise is `steps` contour terraces `h` high whose rings shrink from `r` to `r * top`; `lean: [dx, dy]` offsets each higher ring (steeper on one side). Rises must not overlap. `trRelief(K, P, T)` draws them as contour steps (slope faces plus a tinted top with one contour line at the lip, higher steps warmer, no outline along the foot, so they read as ground, not plates). `trBehind(K, P, T, x, y, pad)` is true where a standing thing would poke through a rise from behind: pass it to every `avoid`. `trDrape(T, pts, lift)` lifts a 2D polyline onto the ground.

**Ground cover** (strings for the ground layer, in this order): `trGroundPlane(K, P, worldRect)` first; `trPatch(K, P, ring, cls)` meadows and wood floors; `trField(K, P, quad | {cx, cy, w, d, ang}, { furrows, tint: "a"|"b" })` with furrows that end on the outline; `trStream(K, P, pts, w, seed)` water, banks and ripples (every line fades at its ends); `trRoad(K, P, pts, w, { ruts, smooth })` farm track or road with fading wheel ruts; then `trRelief`. Run roads and streams well past the visible ground.

**Standing cover** (items `{ key, svg }`, depth-sorted with the troops): `trScatter(ring, n, { seed, gap, r, h, cone, lobes, avoid })` → trees; `trWood(K, P, T, ring, trees)` → `{ floor, items }`; `trTree(K, P, t, z)` broadleaf (round or clumped crown with a shade crescent and a ground shadow) or conifer (`kind: "cone"`); `trHedge(K, P, pts)` and `trWall(K, P, pts)` → items in about 10-unit pieces so each sorts on its own; `trHouse(K, P, x, y, ang, len, wid, eave, ridge)` walls, tinted gabled roof, door and windows on the visible faces.

**Enemy positions and obstacles.** `trHole(K, P, x, y, z, face, { len, deep, heavy })` two-man fighting position (pit, sandbag row and returns) as two items keyed 1.8 either side of its centre, so an occupant keyed at the centre sorts between back and front bags. `trNest(K, P, x, y, z, face)` MG horseshoe, two courses. `trWire(K, P, T, pts, { every, r })` concertina coils on pickets (coils start and end on picket tops). Put occupants in `pose: "hole"`.

**Soldiers.** `trSoldier(K, P, { face, pose, role, stride })` → inner SVG for one figure **at the origin**: draw it inside `<g class="tr-s" data-side="f|h" [data-lit] transform="translate(sx sy)">` where `[sx, sy] = K.iso([x, y, z], P)` (the iso projection is affine, so a world move is a screen translate). Poses: `stand` (with `stride` 0/1/2 for a walk cycle), `kneel`, `prone`, `hole`. Roles: `tl`, `ar` (heavier weapon), `gr` (launcher under the barrel), `r`, `sl`/`pl`/`rto` (antenna), `mg` (heavy, bipod when prone), `ag`. Each body part is a small prism of at most three paths (`trPrism`), so a figure is about a dozen paths. `trLook(spec)` is the cache key (facing rounded to 15°, `trFaceStep`): render each look once and reuse it. `trMuzzle(spec, x, y, z)` and `trAimPoint(spec, x, y, z)` give the world points tracers start and end at. `trVehicle(K, P, face)` a small tracked vehicle (hull with glacis, tracks, turret, gun), same origin convention, tinted by `data-side`.

**Groups and formations** (offsets in the leader's frame, `u` forward, `v` left, interval `s` = 4.2 units, members carry `role`): `trFireTeam(form)` 4 men (team leader, automatic rifleman, grenadier, rifleman) in `wedge` (team leader leads, the rest echeloned behind; ATP 3-21.8 calls the wedge the fire team's basic formation, para 3-46), `file` or `line`; `trSquad(form, teamForm)` 9 men, two teams plus the squad leader, in `column` (team A, SL, team B), `line` or `file` (ATP 3-21.8 para 3-49 lists squad column, line and file; there is no squad wedge); `trPlatoon(form, squadForm, teamForm)` three squads plus PL, RTO and PSG in `column`, `line`, `wedge` or `vee`; `trCompany(kind)` and `trBattalion(kind)` clusters (`kind: "mech" | "armor" | "inf"`, members carry `kind: "veh" | "man"`). `trMorph(a, b, t)` blends two formations of the same members (column into line for an assault). `trPlace(members, { x, y, face })` → world positions. `trRoute(pts)` → `{ length, at(s) → { x, y, face } }`. `trBound(s, L, w, gap)` → `{ a, b, moving }`: two elements alternating bounds of length `L` along a route parameter, `w` blending from travelling together (0) to bounding (1); the one not moving overwatches.

**Marks on the ground.** `trFan(K, P, T, cx, cy, r0, r1, a0, a1, { side, arcs, alpha })` fire sector draped on the ground (closed outline plus range arcs ending on its edges). `trChalk(K, P, T, pts, { closed, pegs, h })` → `{ ground, items }`: a dashed chalk line on pegs, open for a phase line, closed for a support-by-fire position, assault position or battle position. `trObjective(K, P, T, x, y, opts)` flag on a post. Use them sparingly: one phase line, one or two outlines, one flag.

**Fire.** `trShots(streams, t)` → `{ tracers, flashes, puffs, smoke }`, a pure function of time. A stream is `{ side, from, to: [targets], period, burst, gap, flight, phase, on, heavy, smoke, scatter }`: bursts every `period` s, `on` the share of bursts fired (fade fire in and out with it), targets chosen per burst, each round a tracer from the muzzle to a scattered point at the target, then a strike puff and (with `smoke`) drifting smoke. Tracers never start before the muzzle or run past the impact.

**Page runtime.** `trStage(layer)` takes the layer of static `<g data-k>` items written by `fdStatic` (it removes the build's `data-dyn` first frame) and returns `put(list)`, list items `{ id, key, at: [sx, sy], look, svg: () => string, side, lit }`: one `<g>` per id, re-rendered only when `look` changes, moved by `transform`, and re-inserted among the statics only when its slot changes. `trFire(group, K, P)` builds pools of tracer, flash, puff and smoke nodes in a `<g class="tr-fire">` drawn above everything and returns `draw(shots)`, which writes attributes only.

## Recipe: retrofit a figure's Battlefield view

1. **Scale and reading.** Pick the scale from the table above and write the doctrinal picture in one sentence: who fixes, who maneuvers, where the objective is, what changes when the model's state changes. Source any formation or composition claim (ATP 3-21.8, 2024 C1, is opened and cited in `flank-attack/FACTCHECK.md`; FM 3-90 for tasks).
2. **`scripts/field-scene.mjs`** with four exports, importing from `../../kit/field.mjs` and `../../kit/terrain.mjs`:
   - a layout object (`FG`) with the world rect, the rises (`FG.TERRAIN`) and every notional number;
   - `fieldTerrain()` → `trTerrain(FG.TERRAIN)` (build and page both call it);
   - `fieldStatic(K, P, T)` → `{ ground, items }`: ground plane, patches, fields, stream, roads, wood floors, relief and chalk lines in `ground`; trees, hedges, walls, houses, holes, nests, wire, pegs and the flag in `items`;
   - `fieldPose(S, GEO, T, M)` → `{ men: [{ id, side, lit, role, pose, face, stride, x, y, z }], streams, … }` from the **same state `S`** the machine's loop updates (never a second model). `M` carries what the page measures between frames (speeds, eased positions); give it defaults so the build's first frame works without it. Derive `stride` from distance travelled (`[1, 0, 2, 0][floor(dist / 1.7) % 4]`), so feet move only while a unit moves;
   - `fieldMarks(K, P, T, pose)` → fans (and any other per-state ground marks).
3. **Layout rules.** Keep the action in the middle 70% of the card and quiet: woods and copses round it, fields, farms and hedges toward the edges. Keep a corridor at least 15 units either side of every route through woods, and smaller trees on the wood's near (viewer) side. No trees or houses just behind a rise (`trBehind`). Put enemy positions on a plateau wide enough that no higher terrace hides an occupant. Exactly one lit element: the main effort.
4. **`build.mjs`.** Use a viewBox the shape of the card (Fig 1: 640 × 420) and `PF = k.fitProjection(<four ground points that frame the visible rect>, FW, FH, { pad: 0, azimuth: 55, elevation: 42 })`. Body: `<g data-part="ground">` + `<g data-part="marks">` + `<g data-part="field-live">${fdStatic([...items, ...firstFrameMen])}</g>` + `<g data-part="fire" class="tr-fire"></g>`, where each first-frame man is `{ key: depthOf([x, y, 0]), dyn: true, svg: <g class="tr-s" …transform>trSoldier(…)</g> }`. Add `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` to the field SVG. Inline `kit/field.mjs`, `kit/terrain.mjs`, then `field-scene.mjs`; append `FIELD_CSS + TERRAIN_CSS` after `ISO_CSS`.
5. **Page CSS (full bleed).** Hide the machine SVG with `visibility:hidden` in the field view so the card keeps its height, and lay the field SVG over the whole card: `.fl-field{position:absolute;inset:0;width:100%;height:100%;overflow:hidden;mask-image:<two linear gradients, about 7% at the sides and 12% top and bottom>;mask-composite:intersect}` (plus the `-webkit-` forms), `.iso-plate-corners{z-index:2}` and a card-coloured `text-shadow` on the corner text in the field view. A phone crops the sides, which zooms the middle.
6. **Live code.** Keep the toggle UX from FIELD.md unchanged (buttons, V key, `localStorage["maneuvers.view"]`, `setView` saving and calling `draw()`, then `run()`). Add `drawField()`: create `trStage(fieldLayer)` and `trFire(fireEl, K, PF)` once; each frame `stage.put(pose.men.map(m => ({ id, key: depthOf([m.x, m.y, 0], PF), at: iso([m.x, m.y, m.z], PF), look: trLook(m), side, lit, svg: cached trSoldier })))`, redraw `marks` only when its inputs change, and `fire.draw(trShots(pose.streams, t))`. Advance `t` by `dt` in the tick; in reduced motion pass a fixed `t` for a still frame. Keep the loop running while the field view is visible and motion is allowed (fire never settles), and stop it offscreen as before.
7. **Page text.** One "Battlefield view" paragraph in "How to read it": which element is which and its composition, formations, what changes when the state changes, and that sizes, ranges, timings and figure scale are notional. Quote formations and compositions only from a source you opened (ATP 3-21.8 paras 1-9, 1-11, 3-46, 3-49, 3-54, 3-91; FM 3-90 for support by fire), and add FACTCHECK rows marked "UNVERIFIED: pending independent check". If you draw map symbols at all, cite MIL-STD-2525D, Tables I, D-III, D-IV (see the builder brief); Fig 1 uses none.
8. **Verify** (one headless Chrome at a time): `lines.mjs` only reads the visible view, so drive it: `["eval", "window.__x.stop(); window.__x.setView('field')"]`, `["lines"]`, a second pose (state changed and settled, then `stop()` again because `steer` restarts the idle timer), `["lines"]`, then `["key", "v"]` and the machine view's two poses. All must print `line ends: 0`. Tracers, rifles and antennas are slender objects (class `tr-tracer`, `tr-gun`, `tr-wire`), not texture lines; every `iso-line` (furrows, chalk, fans, contours, wire coils, openings) must end on something or fade. Console clean, no sideways scroll at 390 px, `model.test.mjs`, 4× close-ups of soldiers, positions and tracer ends, a phone shot. Frame timing: preload a `requestAnimationFrame` wrapper that records callback time and timestamps, run the tour in the field view for 6 s, and report fps and JS p50/p95 at desktop and at 390 px with a 4× CPU throttle (Fig 1: 60 fps both; JS 0.4/1.0 ms desktop, 1.5/3.3 ms throttled phone).

## Budget

Fig 1's field view has about 3,900 static paths (trees about 4 each, a soldier about 12) and 45 soldiers; the page is 1.15 MB raw, 234 KB gzipped. Per frame it sets about 45 transforms and up to 60 tracer and puff attributes; soldiers re-render only when their look changes (stride or a 15° facing step). Stay under about 60 soldiers and 5,000 static paths per figure, and avoid CSS `filter` (blur) in the field view: it repaints every frame.
