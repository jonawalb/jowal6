# Movement to Contact: fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check (fresh context; all three official PDFs re-downloaded from armypubs.army.mil and text-searched). Claims 1-22 quotes, paragraph numbers and printed page numbers (checked against running footers) match; claim 23 matches ATP 3-21.8 1-9 and 3-46; no distance or time found in the cited paragraphs (claim 21). Page does not say "advance guard" (ATP 3-21.10 2-62/2-63 and ATP 3-21.8 4-68 do use it; the page avoids it). Notional labelling present on page, captions, aria-labels and aria-valuetext. Minor, not errors: the visible readout ("3 vs 6 down", "Enemy position defeated") carries no "notional" tag itself (aria-valuetext and the page do); the legend line "Enemy: six defenders" is not tagged (page and field aria-label are).

Notes for the checker. The page says the figure "shows only an attack after contact" (FM 3-90 4-1 also lists a hasty defense and bypass; 3-4 lists five options). The lead element drawn is a squad; ATP 3-21.10 2-62 says a single platoon normally serves as the forward security force in a company, and the page says the rest of that platoon is not drawn. The page does not call the lead squad the "advance guard".
