          How to read it

          The chain runs top to bottom, from design software to the finished, tested accelerator. Each bar splits one stage's world output by country, with the data year and source beside it. Shares come from different sources and years, so compare them with care. The controls overlay counts the U.S., allied and Chinese measures aimed at each stage; the timeline below lists them with dates and links to the official text.

          Pick a shock in the side panel. The output lost at each stage is hatched, and the simulator shows how much world AI accelerator output the chain can still support, month by month. The narrowest stage binds. Buffers delay the loss, and substitutes restore output after a time you set. The simulator runs a fast and a slow substitution case side by side; the amber band between them is the part that depends on how fast substitutes arrive.
