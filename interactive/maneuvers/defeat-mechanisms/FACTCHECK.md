# Defeat Mechanisms: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (ADP 3-0 2019 paras 2-11 to 2-15, 2-19, 2-20, checked on rendered pages; FM 3-0 2022 paras 3-106, 3-110, 3-112). All 14 claims confirmed, each attributed to the right edition. Fixed after the check: the lede no longer says "rather than one at a time"; the FM 3-0 changes are "some changes, including these two"; the dilemma term is described as a multiplier..

Notes for the checker:
- ADP 3-0's text layer drops paragraph numbers; they were read from rendered page images (PDF pages 28 and 29 of the archived file). FM 3-0's numbers are in its text layer (PDF pages 77 and 78).
- FM 3-0's isolate definition (3-111) cites ADP 3-0 and matches it; its destroy wording differs slightly ("apply lethal force against an enemy capability so that it can no longer perform its function", 3-109). The page quotes the ADP 3-0 wording for both and says FM 3-0's wording "stays close to ADP 3-0, with two notable changes" (dislocate, disintegrate).
- Not doctrinal claims: the capability numbers (70/42/24/13%, and 49/34/24% without the dilemma term), the equal weighting, the bench's mapping of a link plate to command and control, and the tool order are notional and labeled so on the page.
