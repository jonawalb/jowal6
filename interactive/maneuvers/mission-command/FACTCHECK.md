Note for the checker: the 2026 Introduction says the 2012 edition made mission command a warfighting function and system, and that the 2019 and 2026 editions use "command and control" as the term, with mission command as the approach. The page only says the edition was retitled and keeps mission command as the approach; it does not claim the principles changed (the seven are the same).

Not doctrinal claims: every engine number (speeds, lags, gains, crank rate, the 70% / 97% / 100% / 102% / 140% values) is a notional model, labeled as such on the page and in METHOD.md. The engine-to-unit mapping is an analogy and the page says so.
