# Fix and Flank: fact check

Checked 2026-10-13 by a separate fresh-context agent against the official PDF:
Headquarters, Department of the Army, FM 3-90, *Tactics*, 1 May 2023.
https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN38160-FM_3-90-000-WEB-1.pdf

| Claim on the page | Verdict | Source text |
|---|---|---|
| Five forms of maneuver: frontal attack, penetration, envelopment, turning movement, infiltration | Confirmed | Para 2-24 and Table 2-1, p. 2-13 |
| Earlier doctrine also named the flank attack | Confirmed | 2023 terms table: "flank attack: No longer used as a defined Army term"; six-form list in FM 3-90-1 (2013) |
| Envelopment: "an attacking force avoids an enemy's principal defense by attacking along an assailable flank" | Confirmed, exact | Para 2-58, p. 2-19 |
| "the enemy's front—where the effects of enemy fires and obstacles are generally the greatest" | Confirmed, exact substring | Para 2-58 |
| Units prefer it "because generally the flanks of the defending forces are weaker" | Confirmed, exact substring | Para 2-58 |
| "supporting efforts that fix the bulk of the enemy's forces in their current positions" | Confirmed, exact | Para 2-68, p. 2-21 |
| Fix: prevents "the enemy from moving from a specific location for a specific period"; "always a supporting effort" | Confirmed, exact (2023 modified definition) | Para B-35, p. B-8 |

Corrections made after the check:
- The first draft said doctrine lists six forms, including the flank attack, and quoted a flank-attack definition. The 2023 manual lists five and retired the term; the page now says so.
- The first draft paraphrased "fix" with the older wording ("any part of its force"). It now uses the 2023 text.
- Doctrine says a fixed enemy is held in place. It does not say the enemy "cannot turn to face" the maneuver. The page attributes the turning picture to the bench model, not to doctrine.

Not a doctrinal claim: the fire-share curve and the turn rate are notional and labeled as such on the page.

## Added 2026-10-13 with the terrain Battlefield view (platoon scale)

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker, with these fixes applied to the page:
- Title given as ATP 3-21.8, *Infantry Rifle Platoon and Squad*, 11 January 2024, incorporating Change 1, June 2025.
- "Four-man teams" removed (no source gives a size); the team is now "a team leader, an automatic rifleman, a grenadier and a rifleman" (para 1-11, p. 1-4).
- The released-state movement is now described as "fire and movement" (para 1-9), not bounding overwatch (para 3-91 applies bounding overwatch when there is no enemy contact); "prone" dropped.
- "Fix" stays sourced to FM 3-90 (above); support by fire is now quoted from ATP 3-21.8 para 3-56, p. 3-17.
