## Removed because the 2026 text does not support them
- Asterisks on steps 4, 5, 6 and the "occur simultaneously" statement (2018 para 3-146): the 2026 list has none.
- "Is not the only way to develop an engagement area" note (2018): not in the 2026 edition; claim 2 carries the "one way" wording instead.
- Step 3 bullets ("Identify target reference points that align with ...", "establish engagement areas around target reference points"), step 4 stake bullet, step 5 "block, disrupt, fix, and turn ... into planned engagement areas", step 7 "use friendly forces to depict the enemy": none found in the 2026 edition. The page no longer quotes them, and the battlefield soldier who walks the avenue in step 7 is described as a drawing choice, not doctrine.
- "Integrates these steps within the troop leading procedures and the IPB" (2018 para 3-146): not in the 2026 para 3-61.
- All FM 3-90 citations (para 8-52, table 17-1): dropped; the fix definition is re-sourced from ATP 3-21.10 (claim 9). FM 3-90 was not checked for a newer edition because it is no longer cited.

## Not doctrinal claims (labelled notional on the page and in METHOD.md)
Every speed, rate, kill multiplier, distance (trigger line, standoff, belt), the column of twelve elements (six vehicles and six dismounts in the battlefield view), the six soldiers drawn per platoon, three platoons and a mortar section, soldiers at about 1.5 times scale, the battle positions' layout, the losses, the two avenues' geometry, the stakes as drawn marks for TRPs, and the step-by-step build order as a display choice. That the engagement area is built on the wrong avenue when step 1 or 2 is missing, that without step 3 the fire is scattered, and the effect of each other missing step, are the model's own (the page says so), not claims in the manual.
