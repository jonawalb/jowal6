# Screen, Guard and Cover: fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check against the official FM 3-90 (1 May 2023), ATP 3-21.10 (1 June 2026) and ATP 3-21.8 (C1) PDFs. Claims 1-31 verified verbatim with running-footer pages. Findings applied: the covering-force scale sentence now cites FM 3-90 13-105 and 13-106 and ATP 3-21.10 5-26; the field aria label keeps 13-56's "engage ... within their means ... otherwise fight only in self-defense"; the lede says FM 3-90 lists four types of security operations (13-51) and the figure shows three.
Verified without change: ladder is stated as the figure's own reading (claim 32); 81+ min, 15/25/37+ min, 3 vs 5 down, 10/20/40 per cent, 13 and 33 min all reproduce from model.mjs and are labeled notional on the page, captions, LABELS and aria-valuetext; "Enemy stopped" is phaseWord() in the model, not doctrine. Minor, optional: the visible readout string itself carries no "notional" (the card caption and aria-valuetext do).

The page does not show area security, flank guard, rear guard or the advance guard's movement to contact. Claim 25: the two ATP 3-21.10 sentences are consecutive in 5-20 (the first ends "...(I)." and the second begins "More distance usually yields..."); confirm the page's join.
