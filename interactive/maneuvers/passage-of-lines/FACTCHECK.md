# Passage of Lines and Relief in Place: fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check against the official FM 3-90 (1 May 2023), ATP 3-21.10 (1 June 2026) and ATP 3-21.8 (C1) PDFs. Rows 1-28 and 37 verified verbatim with running-footer pages. Findings applied: ATP 3-21.10 para 5-81 cited as p. 153; the page now states that only a forward passage and sequential/simultaneous relief are modelled; the transfer-trigger paragraph adds FM 3-90 15-25 and ATP 3-21.10 5-52 conditions and presents "two of three platoons" as the figure's own choice for both operations.
## Independent check 2026-10-14: 3 problems, all fixed (see header) (36 quote rows VERIFIED against official PDFs, see below)

Cover dates confirmed from the official armypubs PDFs: FM 3-90 "01 May 2023"; ATP 3-21.8 "(INCL C1) 11 January 2024"; ATP 3-21.10 "01 June 2026", "supersedes ATP 3-21.10, dated 14 May 2018". Every quoted sentence in rows 1-35 matches wording, paragraph and running-footer page, except as noted.

1. WRONG page cite (row 32 / page ATP 3-21.10 paragraph): para 5-81 is entirely on p. 153 (the p. 152 footer closes after 5-80). Page text "(para. 5-81, pp. 152 and 153)" must read "(para. 5-81, p. 153)". Row 32's note "first of the quoted pair is on p. 153" is also the second; both quotes are on p. 153.
2. MISSING limits: the page never says rearward passage and staggered relief are not modelled. Add to "What is notional": "Only a forward passage is modelled; a rearward passage is not. Of the three relief variations, sequential and simultaneous are modelled (with two at once as the model's own in-between); staggered is not." (Also add to the machine and battlefield captions or LABELS if wanted.)
3. TRIGGER paragraph incomplete: "The sources therefore give three triggers ..." should (a) say FM 3-90 para 15-25 also uses "roughly two thirds of the personnel responsible or security active" plus "sufficient communications" for a simultaneous relief, so FM two-thirds is not passage-only; (b) quote ATP 3-21.10 5-52 in full: "a majority of the fighting force in place and all C2 systems are operating"; (c) say plainly "The figure uses 2 of 3 platoons, its own choice for both operations; for three platoons two thirds and a majority both round to two, and ATP 3-21.8's all elements would be 3 of 3." Currently "one reading, not the only one" does not say 2 of 3 is the figure's choice.

Checked and fine: notional labelling of thresholds, men-down rules, 2 lanes, k, timings and outcomes on page ("What is notional", fine print), CAPTION, FIELD_CAPTION, LABELS.machine/field, aria-valuetext; page numbers (18/9 s, 14/5 s, 14 s with 4 s halt, 22/16/10 s; 1v3, 2v5, 4v5, 1v1, 1v1, 5v2) match model.mjs finalState; model.test passes. Rows 37 (negative claim) holds on keyword scan: no lane count for a company, no time, no detection point in ATP 3-21.10 5-51 to 5-83 or ATP 3-21.8 sect II-III. Minor, optional: the live readout words "Handed over" and "Detected" carry no "in this model" in the visible readout (only in aria-valuetext and captions).
