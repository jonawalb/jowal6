# Deep Battle and Echelons: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (Glantz, "Soviet Operational Art and Tactics in the 1930s", SASO 1990, DTIC AD-A232954, pp. 3, 12–15; Kipp, DTIC AD-A195053, pp. 20–21; Wayback copies). All quotes and numbers confirmed at the right level (tempos for front operations, depths for armies). Fixed after the check: "older than the tanks" became "Echeloned attack predates the tank forces" (Kipp on Frunze); the ranges are attributed to Glantz’s "in theory"; the meta marks the model and the reserve race as notional; the caption calls the regulations quote a quote, not a summary.
