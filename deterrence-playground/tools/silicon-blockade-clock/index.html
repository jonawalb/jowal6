        How to read it

        Taiwan held about 69 percent of the world's sub-10nm logic capacity in 2022. Nvidia, which sold almost half of the world's AI accelerators in 2025 by Epoch AI's estimate, has its data-center chips made by TSMC and packaged with TSMC's CoWoS. A quarantine or blockade reaches that output first through energy. Taiwan imports over 94 percent of its energy, holds about eleven days of natural gas and about six weeks of coal, and electronic-products and electrical-equipment manufacturing used 23 percent of its electricity in 2024. A full blockade also stops chips leaving and process materials arriving.

        Pick a scenario and a duration, then press Play or drag the day slider. The chain at the top shows each link on the selected day: fuel stocks, grid supply, power reaching leading-edge fabs and packaging lines, their output, what gets out of Taiwan, the finished-chip buffer abroad, and world deliveries. The large chart shows where the world's accelerators come from each day, what falls short of normal, and the catch-up above normal once supply returns. The table shows which alternatives ramp up, and how slowly.

        Three things decide the result: whether Taiwan's rationing protects the fabs, whether the blockade also stops chips leaving by air and sea, and how long the buffer of finished chips outside Taiwan lasts. Compare an energy-only blockade with a full blockade of the same length.
