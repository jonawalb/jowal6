# Center of Gravity: fact check

VERIFIED 2026-10-13 by an independent fact-checker (JP 5-0, 1 Dec 2020, Wayback copy of the jcs.mil PDF; Clausewitz, Graham 1873, Wayback clausewitz.com). All quotes are exact. After the check the lede was softened ("the one thing" is now "what") and the multiple-COG sentence now quotes JP 5-0 para 4.c.(1)(a)–(b) directly.

Not doctrinal claims: the crown thrust (2 W), stone weights, the linear loss of bearing, the 12 mm failure point, the lean and the collapse choreography are notional and labeled so on the page. The joint-load formula (vector sum of crown thrust and weight above) is ordinary statics.

Note for the checker: the German term *Schwerpunkt* was deliberately left off the page because no German source was opened this session.
