# Clearing a Trench or Building (Fig 49): fact check

VERIFIED: 2026-10-14, independent check against ATP 3-21.8 (C1), FM 3-90, ATP 3-21.10 (1 June 2026) and ATP 3-06 (July 2022). Verified as sourced: drill names and the statement that steps are not printed (ATP 3-21.8 E-1, E-2, References-4), 1-9, 1-22 (p. 1-10), 3-48, 4-113, FM 3-90 B-16, ATP 3-21.10 2-179, ATP 3-06 3-22. Findings applied: all step quotations from the unofficial 2008-era compilation were removed (it could not be identified as an official publication and one page is marked FOUO); the page now says no drill steps are reproduced and that the sequence is the figure's own; "own sequence / notional" added to the aria labels; the trench-drill-is-a-platoon-drill note added to the field caption; the trench and hall layouts marked as the figure's own. The 2022 drill packets (07-SQD-D9509, 07-PLT-D9510) and ATP 3-06.11 were not opened.
Minor: ATP 3-21.8 para 1-22 starts on p. 1-9; the platoon-medic words are on p. 1-10. Compilation step "5d(4)": the step "5" heading was not extractable, numeral unconfirmed (text of d(4), d(6) verified on pp. 13-14).
